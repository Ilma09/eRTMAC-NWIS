5.1 CEMENT QUALITY NOTE
Cementing objectives included zonal isolation, casing support and pressure containment across the wellbore.
Class G cement was selected with additives suited to the anticipated downhole thermal environment.
Lead and tail slurry systems were used to establish the required cement column behind the casing.
Fluid loss control was incorporated to limit slurry filtration into permeable intervals during placement.
Retarder treatment supported controlled thickening time during deeper cementing stages.
The 13-7/8 inch surface casing received separate lead and tail slurry stages for improved placement control.
The intermediate string was cemented with a lighter lead system followed by the planned displacement sequence.
The production string used a single-tail design extending toward the principal completion interval.
Cement volumes and density were reviewed against casing geometry and planned top-of-cement requirements.
